Choosing a role

Not sure which fits? Postdocs lead independent research. PhD students dive deep into a topic over 3-5 years. Master's students build foundations. Interns get hands-on project experience.

A few notes

Use your passport name for official records. Google Scholar helps us quickly find your publication record.

About scores

GPA scales vary by country — include yours (e.g., 3.8/4.0). For English: IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 90+ is preferred.

Document tips

Keep your CV concise (2-3 pages). For the pitch deck: 3 slides covering Problem, Approach, and Expected Impact.

We review essays and CVs carefully. Please invest personal time to make this your best work. AI tools are fine, but if we find artifacts suggesting a lack of preparation, we may decline the application to save everyone's time.

Research vision

Pick 1-3 areas that genuinely interest you. The AI plans question is about your methodology — how you'll integrate AI into your actual research workflow.

Good to know

KFUPM provides housing for researchers and families. Visa processing typically takes 4-8 weeks. Start dates are flexible.
